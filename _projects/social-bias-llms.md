This project investigates the mechanisms behind social bias in large language models, separating the effect of a prompt's linguistic framing from the effect of explicitly specifying a social group. The goal is to better understand when and why model outputs diverge across groups.

**Authors:** Vishwanath Emani Venkata, Silvia Téliz

Presented at [IC2S2 2026](https://ic2s2-2026.org/program/) (International Conference on Computational Social Science), University of Vermont, Burlington, VT, July 28–31, 2026.

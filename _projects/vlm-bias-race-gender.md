Do Vision Language Models' (VLMs) zero-shot classifications of race and gender reflect the viewpoint of a specific demographic subgroup? To address this question, we propose a novel audit framework that compares the labeling of human face images across different VLMs with annotations from a demographically balanced sample of U.S. adults comprising two racial groups (White and Black) and two gender groups (male and female). This design will allow us to identify which social group's classifications most closely resemble each model's outputs.

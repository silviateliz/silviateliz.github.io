My dissertation examines how skin tone and other visual cues influence electoral outcomes in Mexico, using urban visual data and computer vision to study out-of-home political advertising in federal electoral campaigns.

Committee: Dr. Sandra González-Bailón (chair), Dr. Deen Freelon, Dr. Yphtach Lelkes.

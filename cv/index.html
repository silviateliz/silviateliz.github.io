I am a PhD candidate (expected July 2027) at the Annenberg School for Communication, University of Pennsylvania, studying visual political communication and social biases in multimodal AI systems. I combine computer vision, natural language processing, and machine learning to analyze large-scale multimodal data, from TikTok content, to street political advertisements. My dissertation project, Visual Cues as Political Communication: A Computational Look into Mexican Federal Electoral Campaigns, examines how skin tone and other visual cues influence electoral outcomes in Mexico. I have also conducted research on the nature of social biases in large language models and visual language models. My work has been published in the International Journal of Press/Politics and presented at conferences such as IC2S2 and PolNet-PACSS. I have extensive experience in teaching and mentoring students, having served as a teaching fellow for two courses at the Annenberg School for Communication, and as an instructor of record at Universidad Panamericana.
